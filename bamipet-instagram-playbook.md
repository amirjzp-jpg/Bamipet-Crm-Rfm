# Part One — The 2026 Algorithm: What Actually Matters

Instagram runs **four separate ranking systems** — Feed, Reels, Stories, Explore — each weighing different signals. But five signals matter across all of them, in this order:

| Rank | Signal | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Sends / Shares** | Private DM shares. **The single strongest signal in 2026** — weighted 3–5x higher than likes for reaching new audiences. The test for every post: *would someone DM this to a specific friend?* |
| 2 | **Watch Time / Completion** | The most important signal specifically for Reels. A 15-second Reel at 80% completion outperforms a 3-minute Reel at 20% completion. Length alone is not an advantage — length + retention is. |
| 3 | **Saves** | Roughly 3x the weight of a like. Signals lasting value — content people want to return to. |
| 4 | **Likes per Reach** | Still matters, but carries less weight than the above. A post seen by 1,000 people with 50 likes outranks one seen by 10,000 with 100 likes. |
| 5 | **Story Engagement** | New in 2026: Story interaction (replies, reactions, poll taps) now directly influences Feed ranking for that follower. An active Story presence lifts your Feed visibility too. |

## Two Non-Negotiable Warnings

**⚠️ Originality is now enforced algorithmically.** Instagram uses an "Originality Score" to detect recycled or reposted content — including TikTok reposts with watermarks. Accounts posting 10+ reposts within 30 days are excluded from Explore and Reels recommendations entirely. **Every piece of Bamipet content must be made for Instagram, not recycled.**

**⚠️ Instagram is prioritizing "raw, real human content" over AI-generated material in 2026.** Instagram's head confirmed this shift at the end of 2025. This directly shapes Part Five: AI tools are for supporting graphics, never a replacement for real photography of real guardians and companions.

## Practical Rules

- **Hashtags:** 3–5 precise, relevant tags. Posts with 20-30 hashtags are now read as spam-like; some data shows hashtag-light posts outperforming hashtag-heavy ones.
- **Consistency over volume:** 2–4 high-quality Reels per week beats daily low-quality posting. A missed week hurts less than a weak post.
- **Topical focus:** Stay within the five content pillars. Instagram grants "topical authority" to accounts that consistently cover the same subjects — the algorithm rewards specialists over generalists.
- **Respond fast:** DM and comment response speed strengthens the "relationship score" between Bamipet and a follower, which increases how often they see future posts. This is the algorithmic expression of the "کنارِ تو هستم" voice trait.

# Part Two — Format Specifications

## Reels

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Format | 9:16 vertical (1080×1920) |
| Hook window | First 1–3 seconds — up to 50% of viewers drop off by second 3 |
| Hashtags | 3–5 |

**Length by goal:**
- **7–15 seconds** — quick hooks, trending-audio moments, fast tips
- **15–30 seconds** — quick educational content
- **30–90 seconds** — tutorials, storytelling, deeper how-to
- **Up to 3 minutes** — only for genuinely substantial educational content with strong pacing; length without retention hurts more than it helps

**Production rules:**
- Captions/on-screen text are mandatory — most viewers watch with sound off.
- Keep faces and key text inside the center "safe zone" so UI elements and captions don't obscure them.
- Prefer original audio or audio trending under ~5,000 uses; always verify "Original Audio" labeling for business accounts.
- No slow intros, no title cards before the hook.

**Bamipet hook formula (4 patterns):**
1. **Worry question** — "چرا گربه‌ات...؟"
2. **Contradiction** — "فکر می‌کنی مشکله؟ نیست."
3. **Before/after** — a visible transformation
4. **Real moment, no preamble** — cut straight into the scene

## Carousels

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Slides | Up to 20 |
| Engagement | Highest average engagement rate of any format in 2026 |
| Best for | آموزشی (educational) content |

- Each swipe counts as a distinct engagement event.
- If a viewer doesn't swipe to the end, Instagram treats the unseen slides as "new content" and re-surfaces the carousel in their Feed — a well-built carousel effectively gets shown twice.
- The first slide needs the same hook discipline as a Reel's first 3 seconds, just in text/visual form.
- One idea per slide; show the slide number to encourage swiping.
- Ideal content: step-by-step guides, comparisons, "5 signs that...", breaking down one educational topic.

## Stories

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Frequency | 3–7 per day |
| Lifespan | 24 hours |
| Effect | Now directly lifts Feed ranking for engaged followers |

- Use interactive stickers (poll, question box, slider) — these directly strengthen the relationship/closeness score with each follower.
- Good uses: behind-the-scenes, reminders pointing to feed posts, answering DM'd questions, polling on upcoming content.
- Organize Highlights by content pillar (e.g., "آیا طبیعیه", "محصولات", "سگ") so profile visitors can navigate by interest.

# Part Five — AI-Assisted Visual Content Creation

## The Boundary, Before Any Prompt

Instagram's 2026 algorithm favors "raw, real human content" over AI-generated material, and Daily Paws (one of your own references) explicitly bans AI-written content. Combined with Bamipet's own honesty principle:

> **AI tools are for graphics, backgrounds, illustration, and ideation support — never a replacement for real photography of guardians and companions, and never presented in a way that implies it's real.**

This is not just an algorithm-compliance rule — it's the same honesty standard already baked into the brand voice.

## Tool Map — 2026

| Need | Recommended Tool | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Styled product photography | **Nano Banana Pro** (Google/Gemini) | Most physically-accurate lighting/materials; native 4K |
| Text-bearing graphics (quote cards, banners) | **GPT Image 2** or **Ideogram 3** | Best text rendering accuracy — but not for Farsi (see below) |
| Creative/concept mood art | **Midjourney v7/v8** | Strongest aesthetic sense for inspiration and concepting |
| Brand icon/vector sets | **Recraft v3** | Native SVG output, directly usable in Figma |
| Short concept/background video | **Veo 3.1** (Google) | Best photorealism and temporal consistency |

## ⚠️ Farsi Text in AI Images

**No current AI image model reliably renders Persian/Arabic script** — all are trained and benchmarked primarily on Latin text. **Correct workflow:** generate the image or background with AI *without* any text, then add Farsi text as a real text layer in Figma or Canva using Vazirmatn. Never rely on an AI model to render the Farsi copy itself.

## Ready Prompt Templates

**1. Educational carousel background (no text):**

Calm cream (#FAF9F6) base with a subtle Bamipet blue (#1C48C1) geometric accent in one corner, rounded soft shapes echoing a minimalist dog silhouette motif, plenty of negative space for text overlay, no text, no watermark, flat warm lighting, gentle and reassuring mood, 4:5 aspect ratio.

**3. Conceptual illustration (for "is this normal?" educational posts):**
> Warm, gentle flat illustration of a cat curled up sleeping peacefully, soft rounded shapes, warm cream and honey-amber color palette with one Bamipet-blue accent, calming and reassuring mood, minimalist style, no text, no realistic photography, soft diffused lighting.

**4. Short video background (Reel intro/outro card):**
> Slow, gentle camera pan across a softly lit warm cream room with natural window light, minimal and calm, no people, no text, no fast motion, soothing ambient mood suitable for a pet-care brand intro, 9:16 vertical, 3 seconds.

## Pre-Use Checklist

Before using any AI-generated visual, confirm:
- [ ] Does it look realistic enough to be *mistaken* for something it isn't? (It shouldn't.)
- [ ] Has it replaced a real customer/companion photo that should have been used instead? (It shouldn't.)
- [ ] Does the color and mood match `bamipet-visual-guidelines.md`?
- [ ] Is any Farsi text added as a real layer afterward, not generated by the model?
